CSV Editor: about

About

The CSV Editor is a simple tool made by Open Innovations. The aim is to create a basic CSV editor that uses as little bandwidth as possible. As such, it won't have all the bells and whistles of more fully-fledged CSV editors (GRIST, Google Sheets, Excel etc). But that also means it should load incredibly quickly regardless of your connection.

Because we actually care about privacy, it works entirely in the browser; none of the data from your CSV file leaves your device. We don't host your data; you deal with that.
